IRLZ44N pinout: Hold it with the printed label facing you and pins pointing down. Left to right the pins are G — D — S (Gate, Drain, Source). The metal heat-sink tab on the back is electrically connected to the Drain pin — be careful not to short it to anything.
Critical breadboard fact: Rows a–e and rows f–j are NOT connected to each other, even in the same column. The centre gap separates them. The MOSFET drain (column 16, bottom half) can't reach Node A (column 35, top half) on its own — you need a jumper wire (the red one in the diagram) to bridge across.
Keep LC tank leads short: Keep the legs of C1, C2 and the trimmer, and the wires to them, as short as the 10-column span allows. Long flying leads add a little stray inductance and capacitance and can shift the resonant frequency slightly.
